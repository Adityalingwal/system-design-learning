# Mission: System Design (HLD + LLD + AI/ML/Voice)

## Why
Aditya wants real, deep system design skill: take any problem — especially AI agent, voice AI, ML and LLM systems — and design and build it himself, choosing components for clear reasons. Interview readiness should follow from that skill, not drive it.

## Success looks like
- Given any problem, he runs the design process on his own: requirements → estimation → API → data model → high-level design → deep dives → bottlenecks, and defends each choice as "X over Y because Z".
- He can write a clear English design doc that explains his choices and trade-offs (his take-home assignments ask for exactly this).
- He can design and code LLD problems in clean, tested Python: entities first, SOLID, the right patterns, thread-safe where needed.
- He can design production AI systems end to end — RAG, agents, LLM gateway, evals, guardrails — and voice agents (latency budget, turn-taking, telephony, scaling).
- Target roles: AI agent engineer, voice AI engineer, ML engineer, AI engineer — at senior level.

## Constraints
- 2-3 hours on weekdays, 7-8 hours on Sundays.
- Explanations in Hinglish; all his written practice (design docs, LLD code, builds) in English.
- 2 years of voice AI industry work, but little formal system design: start from foundations.
- Books only via legal copies or free sources.

## Out of scope
- Staff/principal topics: org design, influence without authority, multi-team strategy.
- Interview cramming (timed rounds, tricks) until everything else is learned — that is the final, separate phase.
